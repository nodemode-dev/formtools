# Demo credits

Nothing in this demo is third-party media. There are no photographs, stock pictures, templates or sample files: every picture in the recording was generated by formtools' own demo generator (`backend/app/tools/demos/photos.py`, seeded numpy and Pillow landscapes, gradients and patterns), and every thumbnail and preview was rendered by the formtools engine. The camera name, dates and file names are invented for the demo.

`assets/recording.js` holds the engine's answers for that folder, recorded by `scripts/demo-record.py`; the three cover pictures on Home are the engine's own covers, re-encoded smaller by the recorder.

The script, stylesheet and fonts in `assets/` are shared with `demos/family` and `demos/bench`. Inside them, besides formtools' own code: Inter and JetBrains Mono (OFL 1.1, @fontsource 5.3.0), three.js 0.187.0 (MIT), pdf.js 6.5.438 with its worker (Apache-2.0), highlight.js 11.12.0 (BSD-3-Clause), markdown-it 15.0.3 and its bundled dependencies (MIT), xterm.js 6.0.0 and its fit addon 0.11.0 (MIT), and @tauri-apps/api 2.12.4 and the clipboard plugin 2.5.0 (Apache-2.0 or MIT), each listed in `NOTICE.md`.

The app opens the same demo from Home > Demos, Help > Demos and the `demo-photos` command, with the same generator, so this note covers it too.
