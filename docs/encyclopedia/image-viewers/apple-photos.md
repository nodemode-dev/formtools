# Apple Photos (editing parity, brief)

Mac and iOS library app with an integrated editor. Proprietary, Apple platforms only. Included only to define editing parity.

## Features

- **Adjust**: exposure, color, white balance, curves, levels, definition, sharpness and more. formtools note: a short "light, color, detail" tab set is enough for a viewer. (single source)
- **Filters and styles**: preset looks and Photographic Styles. formtools note: skip presets in v1.
- **Crop and straighten**: reframe and straighten; with Apple Intelligence also extend the frame and change perspective. formtools note: crop plus straighten plus aspect presets is parity; perspective is stretch.
- **Retouch and Clean Up**: Retouch fixes small blemishes and dust (not on video). Clean Up removes distracting objects by brushing, needs Apple Intelligence, with possible regional limits, and brushing over a face can blur it. formtools note: same brush-to-erase UX as Windows Repair; warn about faces. (single source)
- **Red-eye**: one-tool removal. formtools note: include if the audience prints or shares flash photos.
- **Non-destructive editing**: originals are kept, edits are copyable to other photos (not crop, retouch or red-eye), revert to original at any time, Command-Z for last step. formtools note: sidecar edit stack with revert is the model; copy-paste adjustments across a selection is a strong batch feature.

## Not verified
