# XnSoft legacy and web products

## XnPocket and XnSmartphone
Pocket PC / Windows Mobile viewer. Download folder holds builds for ARM, MIPS, SH3 and XScale (with French variants) and smartphone CABs; 2008-07-23 builds and version 1.40 dated 2010-05-03. Nothing newer; discontinued in practice (inference from listing, single source). No feature list found.
formtools note: nothing to learn here beyond confirming the viewer lineage.

## Online Convert (convert.xnview.com)
Browser batch resize and convert. Limits: 40 MB per file, 20 images per free batch; paid plan removes the cap. Resize by percent or pixels with aspect lock; presets for web, social, e-commerce and email; listed formats include JPEG, PNG, WebP, JPEG XL, TIFF, HEIF/HEIC, PSD, GIF, BMP, ICO, ICNS, PDF and RAW (how this squares with the stated JPEG/JPG/PNG upload restriction is unclear). Storage and deletion policy not described on the page.
formtools note: the free-batch-of-20 limit is a marketing choice; a local app need not copy it, but presets by destination (web, social, email) are worth copying.
