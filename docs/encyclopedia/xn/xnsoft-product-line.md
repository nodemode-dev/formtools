## Items from the research brief that do not exist as separate current products

- XnView Photo / XnPaint: no standalone product found. "paint" exists as an add-on bundled with XnView Classic (Standard and Extended installers).
- XnPhotoFX: exists only as the iOS app "XnView Photo Fx Editor" (v2.39, 2019).
- Screen capture tool: no standalone tool; capture is a feature inside XnView (Classic and MP).
- Plugins pack: part of XnView Classic downloads (see xnview-classic.md). A dedicated plugin page could not be fetched.

## Licensing summary (site-wide)

XnView and XnConvert are freeware for private, educational and non-profit use. Companies must buy per-seat licenses (from 29.00 EUR for one seat, down to 3.90 EUR per seat above 1000). XnShell is stated as freeware. XnRetro and XnSketch pages state no licence terms.

## Not verified

No discontinuation dates are published by XnSoft; "discontinued" labels here are inferred from last file dates. Release dates of Classic 2.52.x beyond 2.52.2 (2025-06-22, forum post) were not found.
