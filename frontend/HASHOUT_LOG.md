## v0.6.0 · 2026-11-03 · Disk usage heatmap and types

Files: `ui/utilities/diskusage.ts` (switch and wiring), `heatmap.ts`, `types.ts`, `categories.ts` (category list, palette reader, dominant type, gradient), `treemap.ts` (`nest`, beside the existing `squarify`), `io/util-api.ts` (types only), `styles/utilities.css`. The engine side is in `backend/app/tools/utilities/HASHOUT_LOG.md`.

Views, all built once and switched with `hidden`:
- A segmented switch Folders | Heatmap | Types sits in a fixed row beside the breadcrumbs. Switching only toggles `hidden` on three panes that share one grid cell, so nothing is rebuilt (measured: 0 nodes removed from the stage over six switches). The size list on the right stays in every view.
- Folders is the old view. Each block carries `data-cat` (its category, or its dominant category for a folder) which tints it through `--cat`, and a 4 px bar along its bottom edge: one `--tbar` custom property holding a hard-stop `linear-gradient` of its types. Rows in the size list get a swatch and a share bar in the same category colour. The selectors verify4 uses (`.ft-du-cell`, `.ft-du-cell.is-dir`, `.ft-du-crumb`, `.ft-du-map`) are unchanged.
- Heatmap: `nest()` lays out the current folder recursively with `squarify`, 2 px padding per folder level, a 14 px label band on first-level folders large enough for a name, and stops splitting below 12 px. Leaves are files, folded "Other" nodes, folders at the scan's depth limit and folders too small to split. Cushion shading follows van Wijk and WinDirStat: each tile adds a parabolic ridge (height 0.38, falling by 0.91 per level) to its parent's surface, and each leaf pixel is lit with ambient 0.2 plus 0.85 of the cosine to the light (-1, -1, 10). A layout pass fills two typed arrays at device resolution: the shade of every pixel and the tile it belongs to. A paint pass then writes one 32-bit word per pixel from a lookup of category by shade, so a hover or filter change only rebuilds a 13 by 256 table and one pass over the pixels. Folder borders are drawn after, 2.5, 1.75 then 1 device-independent pixel by depth in the background colour with falling opacity. The canvas is sized by its container (`ResizeObserver`, device pixels from `devicePixelRatio`) and is redrawn in one `requestAnimationFrame` only when the folder, the size, the theme (a `MutationObserver` on the root and body class and style) or the highlighted or filtered category changes. A hidden canvas does nothing and catches up when shown.
- Hover: a hit test reads the tile id under the pointer from the id array (no scan of tiles). The tooltip is one DOM node whose text is written only when the tile changes and which is moved with `transform`; a second node outlines the tile (position by transform, size by custom properties). Names and paths from disk go through `textContent` only. Click enters the first-level folder under the pointer and pushes it on the same trail the breadcrumbs use, so the crumbs, the size list and the Folders view follow.
- Legend: 13 fixed rows (swatch, name, share, bytes), fixed order so a folder change never moves a row; categories absent from the folder dim. Hover dims the other categories on the canvas; click filters (others fade almost to the background) and click again clears. The legend and the canvas are the only things that change on hover.
- Types: built once from `stats` of the root result, so it shows the whole scan whichever folder is open. Totals tiles (apparent, on disk with its share of apparent, files, folders, unreadable folders, average file, newest, oldest) and a note saying hard links are counted once and whether on disk is blocks or 4 KB rounding; a stacked bar of 13 segments sized with `flex-grow`; the category table (13 fixed rows); the top 40 extensions and the 50 largest files through `patchList` with show-in-folder.

Palette. Defined once in `utilities.css` as `--ft-cat-*` on `:root` (dark) and `body.theme-light`, mapped to `--cat` by `[data-cat]`; the canvas reads them from computed style. Found by simulated annealing over CIE LCh candidates that already pass 3:1 against the canvas background, maximising the smallest CIEDE2000 distance between any two of the 13 under normal vision (distances scaled by 0.8) and Machado 2009 protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia at full severity. Dark: images #75f2f6, raw #2ba888, video #ae4bcd, audio #9e90fb, documents #f1bd88, code #89b334, data #cce329, archives #c5541f, 3d #6c7795, fonts #bfdfc6, apps #418276, system #867f56, other #bea6c3. Light: images #2f37aa, raw #8441e8, video #f92b14, audio #8378c0, documents #47658a, code #5f850f, data #4e8e61, archives #9f0802, 3d #811764, fonts #904f64, apps #204e46, system #4d623d, other #927084.
- Check (script kept outside the repo): smallest pairwise CIEDE2000 across the 13, dark 13.7 normal, 11.7 protan, 11.0 deutan, 12.5 tritan (worst pair archives and system under deuteranopia); light 12.0, 9.4, 9.4, 9.7 (worst documents and 3d under deuteranopia). Contrast against `--bg-primary`: dark at least 4.11 on all 13 dark themes (worst #101418 Slate; default #111111 4.19); light at least 3.25 on #ecebe6 (Light), 3.18 on #efe8da (Sand), 3.12 on #e9e4f7 (Duck). The 90s skin (#c0c0c0) reaches only 2.13, so it fails 3:1; the legend text and the table carry the names there. A distance near 10 separates the categories, it does not make 13 colours effortless for a colour-blind person, which is why the legend, tooltip and table always say the name.
- Cushion shading multiplies the colour by 0.2 to 1.0, so the 3:1 figure is for the centre of a large tile; the darker slopes are shape cues, not colour.

## v0.3.0 · 2026-11-02 · Phase 2 finish, Phases 3 and 4

- Browser: multi-select (Mod-click toggles, Shift-click and Shift-arrows extend from the anchor, Mod+A all). `selection()` is the picked set, or the focused item. A reload with `keepSelection` restores the focus ring without touching the picked set, because the batch tools reload the folder after a run.
- Ratings and labels follow Lightroom's keys: 0 to 5 rate, 6 to 9 toggle red, yellow, green and blue, in the browser and the viewer. Tags live in XMP sidecars (`name.ext.xmp`) through `/api/meta/*`; the browser reads them in one batch after each render and paints a badge. The badge stars are the `ftStarFill` icon, not the U+2605 character, because the no-emoji gate scans that range.
- Folder tree (`ui/tree.ts`): roots are the engine's places plus "Computer" (`/`) on macOS and Linux, children load on first expand, and reveal expands the deepest unopened ancestor until the open folder is visible. A reveal after a navigation shifts the rows below (measured 0.0003 under throttling); it is the result the navigation asked for, recorded rather than hidden.
- Viewer: "actual pixels" moved from 1 to A, because digits now rate. Crop (R) is an overlay in image coordinates, with aspect presets; dragging inside the box moves it, outside draws a new one. Resize (Shift+R) and Save as open `SaveDialog`, which never replaces the original unless "Replace the original" is chosen, and gives focus back to whatever had it. Pixel inspector (P) samples a decoded copy (at most 40 megapixels; larger pictures are sampled from a scaled copy) and copies the hex on click.
- Media: every video and audio file opens in the viewer; native containers start at once from the raw file; `/api/media/info` decides whether a lossless remux stream (`/api/media/stream`) replaces it. The remux stream cannot seek by byte range; the bar says so. `MediaBar` adds speed, A-B loop (B), frame steps (comma and period, from the stream's fps), J and L, subtitles as `<track>` elements (embedded and beside the file), snapshots and lossless clips and audio extraction through jobs. Audio gets a waveform canvas, drawn once; the playhead and A-B marks move with CSS variables only.
- Viewer grid rows are pinned (`grid-row` 1, 2, 3) because the media bar leaves the grid when hidden, which had collapsed the stage to zero height.
- Tools page (`ui/tools/`): Batch convert, Resize and Rename built once and switched with `hidden`. Action cards are generated from `/api/batch/actions`; the preview reloads 400 ms after the last change and swaps the image only once the new one has decoded. Rename keeps the applied mapping for Undo, because the plan is recomputed after an apply.
- Jobs (`ui/jobs.ts`): a status-bar button and a popover, polled every 400 ms only while a job runs; rows patch in place.
- Toasts use one slot that updates in place: a stack of three moved the first toast when a second appeared (layout shift 0.0009).

## v0.2.0 · 2026-11-02 · viewer and browser (Phase 2)

- Browser: `patchList` keyed by path; thumbnails load through an IntersectionObserver with a 400 px margin and are decoded off-screen before the `img` is shown, so a tile goes from glyph to picture in one write. Tiles reserve a 4:3 box; `content-visibility: auto` keeps large folders cheap.
- Folder loads keep the old listing on screen until the new one arrives; the busy spinner uses `delayedIndicator` (300 ms delay, 250 ms minimum).
- Viewer: each image is fetched and decoded by a probe `Image` first; its natural size sets the pane's transform in the same frame the `src` is set, so there is no size jump. Native formats load the raw file; everything else goes through `/api/image/view`.
- Prefetch keeps the decoded neighbours (Preferences, default 2 each side) in a promise cache keyed by URL; entries outside the window are dropped.
- Zoom anchors at the pointer; double click toggles fit and 1:1; zoom of 200 percent and above switches to pixelated rendering.
- The histogram is drawn from the decoded probe at most 256 px on the long side.
- Rotate and flip write the file through the engine (`/api/image/transform`), lossless for JPEG where the engine can; the viewer bumps a per-path stamp so the new pixels load instead of the cached ones.
- Quick look: Space in the browser opens the viewer in quick mode, where Space closes it again.
